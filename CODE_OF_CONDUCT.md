# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ContextVault to be a welcoming, professional project. Participation should be free from harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disruptive behaviour.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith while asking for clarification where needed.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's documents, credentials, or personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or intimidation.
- Publishing private data, credentials, or confidential documents.
- Deliberately introducing malware, security backdoors, or deceptive benchmark results.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be handled as promptly and discreetly as possible.
